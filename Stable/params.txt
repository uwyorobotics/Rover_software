To make sure the Base station and the Rover are listening and sending on the same ports, 
the ports are all defined here:

TCP ports:
  Rover:
    Listen (command receiver):  9000
    Send    (status/telemetry): 9000  (same connection, reply on accepted socket)

  Base Station:
    Send    (command sender):   9000
    Listen  (status/telemetry): 9000  (same connection, reads replies)

UDP ports:
  Rover:
    Send (video stream):        9002  (stream_id + seq_num in packet header)

  Base Station:
    Listen (video receiver):    9002

IP addressed:
  Rover:
    10.121.111.111 (Example)

  Basestation:
    10.121.111.111
